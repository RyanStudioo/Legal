# **Mezzo Guard Bot Privacy Policy**

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and sharing of personal information by Mezzo Guard Bot, a Discord bot developed by Ryan Studio (ryanstudio). By using Mezzo Guard Bot, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. 

# **Information We Collect** 

We collect the following information:

- Only messages flagged by the model  
- Action taken for each violation  
- Punishment history of members

# **How We Use Your Information**

Messages and punishments will NOT be used to train any AI models in compliance with Discord’s Terms of Service

Messages and punishments may be used to evaluate the models and aid with improving understanding of under and over flags

# **Sharing Your Information** 

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, all models are hosted locally. However, we may disclose your information in response to legal process or a request from a law enforcement agency or regulatory authority. 

# **Data Retention** 

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide Mezzo Guard Bot’s services or as required by law. 

Only server owners and administrators can request data deletion for a guild, individual members cannot request for deletion of punishment or violations in order to provide Mezzo Guard Bot’s services

# **Data Security** 

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information. 

All data is hosted on local hardware and will not be sent to third parties

# **Changes to this Policy** 

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, and we will post the updated policy on our website. Your continued use of Mezzo Guard Bot after we make changes to this policy indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.
